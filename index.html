Who we are

Saudi Nexogen Company installs the HVAC and cooling systems that keep plants, shelters and buildings clean, efficient and healthy. We work inside the safety and quality systems of Saudi Arabia's most demanding operators, with one team accountable from first drawing to handover.

Services

Built to the project specification and the standards it references, including SMACNA, ASHRAE, SBC 501 and client standards such as Saudi Aramco SAES.

How we deliver

Four stages, clear hold points

Every package follows the same sequence, with defined deliverables and client approvals at each stage.

  1. 01

    Engineering & Submittals

    Coordinated shop drawings, material submittals, method statements and inspection and test plans for approval.

  2. 02

    Procurement & Fabrication

    Approved materials from qualified suppliers with full traceability; ductwork and supports made to drawing.

  3. 03

    Installation & Testing

    Work under permit-to-work, daily reports, leak and pressure testing, and commissioning support.

  4. 04

    Handover

    As-built drawings, test records and O&M documentation, with an optional AMC to follow.

Sustainability

Cleaner Solutions. Greener Future.

Efficient HVAC is one of the biggest levers on a Gulf building's energy use. Every system we install is built to waste less, in support of Saudi Arabia's net-zero 2060 goal.

Sealed ductwork. Leakage-tested joints so conditioned air reaches the space instead of the ceiling void.

Insulated piping. Vapour-sealed insulation that holds chilled-water temperature and prevents condensation.

Balanced systems. Support for testing and balancing, so equipment runs at its design efficiency from day one.
